# Consent to the processing of personal data — guest

I consent to the processing by the dormitory of my personal data, for the
purpose of admitting me to the dormitory as a guest and of keeping the visitor
register that clause 2.1.2 of the rules of internal order requires.

**What is processed.** Surname, name and patronymic; the type and number of the
identity document presented at the security post; the room I am visiting and
the resident who invited me; the time I entered and the time I left.

**What is not processed.** No copy, scan or photograph of the document is
taken. The number is stored and shown masked.

**The only ground.** I am not a party to the accommodation contract, so this
consent is the sole ground on which my data may be processed (art. 6 part 1
cl. 1 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ). If I do not give it, or withdraw it, the
dormitory cannot record my entry and I cannot be admitted as a guest.

**Withdrawal.** I may withdraw this consent at the security post at any time.
Withdrawal ends the visit and stops further processing; the record that the
consent was given, and the entries already made in the visitor register, are
kept for the period the local act sets, because the register is what the rules
of internal order oblige the dormitory to keep.
