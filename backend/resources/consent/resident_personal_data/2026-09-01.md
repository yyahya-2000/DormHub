I consent to the processing by the dormitory of the personal data listed below,
for the purposes listed below, on the terms of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of
27.07.2006 «On personal data».

**What is processed.** Surname, name and patronymic; the address the system
writes to; the telephone number; the study status; citizenship; the room and
place assigned to me; the record of my guests' visits.

**What it is processed for.**

1. Sending me messages about events in the dormitory that are not obligatory —
   the decision on a guest request I submitted, and the movement of a
   maintenance request I filed. These rest on this consent alone.
2. Inviting guests: passing to the security post, before a visit, the fact that
   I am the resident who invited the named guest.

**What this consent does not cover.** The keeping of the housing register — the
room, the place, the dates of moving in and out — and the messages the
dormitory is obliged to send me. Those rest on the accommodation contract and
on the rules of internal order, not on this consent, and they continue whether
this consent stands or is withdrawn.

**Withdrawal.** I may withdraw this consent at any time from my personal
account. Withdrawal stops the processing listed above. It does not delete the
record that this consent was given, because the dormitory has to be able to
show on what ground it processed data before the withdrawal (art. 9 part 3).

**Term.** This consent is valid while I live in the dormitory, unless withdrawn
earlier.
