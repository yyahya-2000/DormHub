roboto-cyrillic.ttf is Roboto Regular, subset to the Latin and Cyrillic
characters the account sheet prints. Roboto is (c) Google, licensed under the
Apache License 2.0 — https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The standard fonts of jsPDF are WinAnsi-encoded and have no Cyrillic glyphs, so
a Russian name printed with them comes out as blanks. This file is embedded in
the PDF instead.
